# Промпты: Фоны — Инопланетная планета

## bg_alien_landing.png — Место посадки
A landing site on an alien planet. The shuttle craft sitting on a plateau of smooth crystalline rock. Strange vegetation around the edges — tall bioluminescent trees with spiral trunks, glowing blue-violet leaves. Two moons visible in a twilight sky of deep purple-orange. Mist rising from a valley below. The first human footprints on alien soil. Wonder, beauty, and the unknown.
Style: alien landscape concept art, bioluminescent flora, dual moons, cinematic twilight, ultra detailed, 4K.

## bg_alien_forest.png — Инопланетный лес
A dense alien forest with bioluminescent flora. Trees with translucent trunks showing internal vein-like structures pulsing with light. Mushroom-like growths emitting soft purple glow. Floating seed-spores drifting like tiny lanterns. The ground covered in soft luminescent moss. Alien insects with gossamer wings catching the light. Otherworldly beauty — dangerous and enchanting. Night scene, two moons filtering through canopy.
Style: fantasy-sci-fi forest, heavy bioluminescence, atmospheric particles, magical realism, 4K.

## bg_alien_forest_day.png — Инопланетный лес днём
Same alien forest but in daylight. The amber sun filtering through translucent tree canopies creating rainbow light patterns. Without bioluminescence, the organic architecture of the plants is more visible — fractal patterns, spiral growth, crystalline fruit. Small alien creatures visible — harmless, curious. More welcoming than the night version but still clearly alien.
Style: alien botanical illustration meets concept art, warm amber sunlight, detailed flora, 4K.

## bg_alien_ruins_exterior.png — Руины снаружи
Ancient alien ruins on the planet surface. Massive stone-like structures with geometric precision — not quite pyramids, not quite towers. Surfaces covered in carved symbols that faintly glow. Vegetation has partially reclaimed the structures — alien vines and roots wrapping around columns. A grand entrance with a triangular archway. Scale suggests beings larger or more ambitious than humans. Ancient, mysterious, awe-inspiring.
Style: archaeological sci-fi concept art, ancient meets alien, overgrown ruins, atmospheric mist, 4K.

## bg_alien_ruins_interior.png — Руины внутри
Interior of alien ruins. A vast chamber with impossibly high ceilings. Walls covered in luminescent symbols and star maps. Central pedestal with a dormant holographic projector. The architecture is organic yet precise — curved walls that follow mathematical spirals. Crystals embedded in walls providing soft ambient light. Dust motes floating in light beams from cracks above. Ancient technology sleeping, waiting.
Style: ancient alien temple interior, luminescent symbols, mysterious atmosphere, volumetric light, 4K.

## bg_alien_underground.png — Подземная пещера
A massive underground cavern beneath the ruins. Enormous crystalline formations — stalactites and stalagmites made of an unknown luminescent mineral. An underground lake reflecting the crystal light. Ancient alien machinery integrated into the cave walls — still humming with power after millennia. Walkways carved from the rock, leading deeper. The scale is cathedral-like. Both natural and constructed.
Style: underground crystal cavern, alien tech integration, reflective water, dramatic scale, 4K.

## bg_alien_chamber.png — Палата Луминаров
The deepest chamber — the Luminar sanctuary. A perfect spherical room. Walls covered in swirling patterns of light that respond to presence. Cryo-pods (organic, flower-like) arranged in a circle, most empty, some containing ethereal alien remains. At the center, a column of pure light — the Luminar consciousness interface. Everything pulses with a living heartbeat of light. Sacred, ancient, alive.
Style: alien sacred space, bioluminescent technology, ethereal atmosphere, spiritual sci-fi, 4K.

## bg_alien_campfire.png — Лагерь на чужой планете
An improvised camp site on the alien planet. A portable energy campfire casting warm orange light. The crew's equipment scattered around — scientific instruments, ration containers. The alien forest visible beyond the clearing, bioluminescent trees in the background. Two moons overhead in a star-filled sky. Intimate and vulnerable — humans tiny against the alien world. A moment of rest in the unknown.
Style: sci-fi camping scene, warm vs cool lighting contrast, intimate atmosphere, alien backdrop, 4K.
