## bg_earth_sunrise.png — Рассвет над Землёй (космодром)

## bg_space_battle.png — Космическое столкновение
Space scene with the Aurora and military vessel in confrontation. Energy shields visible as shimmering barriers. Warning lights, debris from a damaged section. The planet visible below. Tension — not a full battle but a standoff. The beauty of space contrasting with human conflict.

## bg_anomaly.png — Космическая аномалия
A massive cosmic anomaly — a swirling vortex of dark energy and light. Not a black hole, not a nebula — something unknown. Tendrils of energy reaching out like fingers. Colors shifting between deep violet, crimson, and absolute black. Stars bending near it. Ancient, powerful, terrifying. The thing the Luminars feared. Scale is incomprehensible.
Style: cosmic horror meets beauty, dark energy vortex, impossible colors, overwhelming scale, 4K.

## bg_energy_field.png — Энергетическое поле Луминаров
Abstract cosmic space — Elara's experience within the Luminar network. Stars, galaxies, nebulae flowing past like a river of light. DNA helices made of starlight. Memories and visions as floating transparent scenes. Neither inside nor outside — a space between dimensions. Beautiful, overwhelming, transcendent.
Style: abstract cosmic art, flowing light, transcendent atmosphere, ethereal, 4K.

The Aurora bridge during crisis — emergency red lighting, sparking consoles, cracked viewscreen. Smoke haze. Some displays still functioning, showing warning data. The ship hurt but not broken. Crew stations in disarray. Urgency and danger.
Style: damaged sci-fi interior, emergency lighting, dramatic tension, debris detail, 4K.
