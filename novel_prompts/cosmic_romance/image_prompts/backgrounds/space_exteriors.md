# Промпты: Фоны — Космос и орбита

## bg_earth_orbit.png — Вид Земли с орбиты
Earth seen from low orbit, the curve of the planet filling the lower third of the frame. Brilliant blue oceans, white cloud formations, green-brown landmasses. The thin blue line of atmosphere glowing at the edge. Sun rising behind the planet creating a golden crescent of light. Stars visible in the black void above. A moment of departure — the last view of home. Emotional, beautiful, melancholic.
Style: cinematic space art, photorealistic Earth, dramatic sunrise lighting, 4K, ultra detailed atmosphere effects.

## bg_deep_space.png — Глубокий космос
Vast empty deep space. Millions of stars of varying brightness and color — white, blue, amber, red. No planets, no nebulae — just infinite darkness peppered with ancient light. The profound emptiness and beauty of interstellar space. A sense of being impossibly far from anything. The silence made visual.
Style: astrophotography-inspired, ultra detailed star field, cinematic depth, subtle color variation in stars, 4K.

## bg_nebula_approach.png — Приближение к туманности
A spectacular space nebula filling the viewport — swirling clouds of gas in purple, magenta, teal, and gold. Newborn stars sparkling within the gas clouds. Pillars of creation-style formations. The Aurora ship (small) approaching the nebula. Scale is overwhelming — the nebula is massive, the ship is tiny. Wonder and intimidation.
Style: NASA-inspired nebula art but more vivid, cinematic composition, volumetric gas clouds, 4K.

## bg_asteroid_field.png — Астероидное поле
Dense asteroid field with rocks of various sizes tumbling through space. Some asteroids catching starlight, others dark silhouettes. Dust and debris creating a haze. Dangerous, chaotic. The ship would need to navigate through this. Orange warning lights reflecting off nearby asteroids. Tension and danger.
Style: cinematic sci-fi, dramatic lighting, debris particles, depth of field, 4K.

## bg_kepler_system.png — Система Кеплер-442
The Kepler-442 star system revealed: an amber-orange star (K-type) casting warm light. Multiple planets visible at varying distances. The impossible planet — lush blue-green, larger than expected — prominent in the foreground. Rings of ice crystals around a gas giant in the background. Two small moons visible near the habitable planet. A star system that feels ancient and full of secrets.
Style: sci-fi space art, warm amber starlight, multiple celestial bodies, cinematic composition, 4K.

## bg_orbit_planet.png — Орбита неизведанной планеты
View from orbit of the alien planet. Swirling cloud patterns unlike Earth's — geometric almost, suggesting atmospheric manipulation. Bioluminescent areas visible on the night side, glowing blue-green. Two moons in different phases. The planet looks alive, welcoming and alien simultaneously. Ocean and landmasses visible but with wrong colors — purple vegetation, turquoise oceans.
Style: sci-fi planetary art, bioluminescent effects, two moons, dramatic orbital view, 4K.
