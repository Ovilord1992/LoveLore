# Промпты: Фоны — Корабль «Аврора»

## bg_aurora_bridge.png — Мостик корабля
A futuristic spaceship bridge interior. Wide panoramic viewscreen showing stars and deep space. Holographic control panels in a semi-circle arrangement, glowing with blue-cyan data displays. Commander's chair at center, elevated slightly. Ambient blue-violet lighting with subtle orange accent lights on consoles. Sleek dark metal surfaces with brushed titanium trim. Floor-to-ceiling supports with integrated displays. The design is functional but elegant — this is humanity's finest ship. Atmosphere of command, control, and infinite space beyond the windows.
Style: cinematic sci-fi concept art, ultra detailed, realistic lighting, 4K, 16:9 aspect ratio, atmospheric, volumetric lighting through viewscreen.

## bg_aurora_corridor.png — Коридор корабля
A long sleek spaceship corridor, slightly curved following the hull shape. Soft recessed LED lighting in floor strips and ceiling panels, casting blue-white glow. Doors on both sides with holographic labels. The corridor is narrow enough to create intimacy for character encounters. Dark composite walls with occasional viewport showing stars. Emergency lighting strips (dormant amber) along walls. Clean futuristic aesthetic.
Style: cinematic sci-fi interior, atmospheric lighting, moody, ultra detailed, 4K.

Workbenches with precision instruments. Multiple screens showing DNA sequences and biological data. Green-tinted accent lighting from bio-growth chambers. Sterile but alive — the intersection of science and wonder. Elara's workspace.
Style: sci-fi laboratory concept art, detailed props, warm-cool lighting contrast, 4K.

## bg_aurora_observation.png — Обзорная палуба
A breathtaking observation deck with a massive curved transparent viewport spanning the entire wall and ceiling. Stars visible everywhere — an ocean of light. Minimal furniture: a few comfortable seats, soft indirect warm lighting from floor. The vastness of space fills the room. A place for contemplation, wonder, and intimate conversations. Reflections of starlight on the polished dark floor.
Style: cinematic sci-fi, awe-inspiring, ultra wide viewport, volumetric star-light, atmospheric, 4K.
